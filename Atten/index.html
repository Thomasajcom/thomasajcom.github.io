One drop.
Chain reaction.

Three modes

Pick your pace.

Classic

Endless run

Every clear charges the board. The grid grows on each jackpot — 4×5, then 5×6, all the way to 9×9. Push your high score until the spawn column locks up.

Daily Puzzle

One shared board

A fresh seed every day for everyone. One attempt per device. Build a streak day over day — and see how your run stacks up on the daily leaderboard.

Infinite

No game-over

Pick your board size, your jackpot rule, your goal cells. The clock doesn't tick. The board doesn't end. Play until you put it down.
