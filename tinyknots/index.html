Why a thread

Not a feed. Not a spreadsheet. A thread.

Baby apps love charts and percentiles. Little Knots is quieter than that. Your family's moments hang in a line you can walk with your thumb — months tied off as little knots, memories resting on it like beads. Tap one and it blooms open into a keepsake, photos, video and all.

What you keep

Everything worth remembering, nothing that isn't.

🌟

71 knots, ready to tie

From Heard the heartbeat to First bike ride — a gentle checklist that knows what tends to come next, and never nags. Anything it doesn't know, you can tie yourself.

🥲

The lasts, too

Nobody tells you when the last stroller ride happens. Little Knots keeps a place for the endings that sneak past — the last bottle, the last nap, the last time they let you carry them.

💬

Tiny philosophers, quoted

A wall for the things they actually say, written in italics the way they deserve. Future-you will be very glad you wrote them down.

🕰️

Late is fine

Real life runs behind. Catch up whenever, child by child — and when you can't recall the exact date, “sometime that summer” is a perfectly good answer.

💞

Tied together Pro

Invite your partner and you both hold the same thread — they tie knots too. Grandparents and godparents can follow along. Everyone sees the same story.

🔒

Yours. Actually yours.

No account. No ads. No tracking — not even analytics. Memories live on your iPhone and sync through your own iCloud. We couldn't peek if we wanted to.

Also in the box: a Home Screen widget, gentle memory reminders, an ink-and-glow dark mode, and confetti where confetti is due.

For the whole crew.
