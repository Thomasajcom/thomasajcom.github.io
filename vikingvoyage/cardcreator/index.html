Card Anatomy

The shared template every card is struck from — three physical stocks, board-game style: leaders large, crew & tactics on one deck, items small. Change it here once — every card follows. Card authors never touch this.

Where the data lives

Everything autosaves to this browser (localStorage). Nothing leaves your machine until you export.

Sharing between designers

Export JSON downloads the whole chest — anatomy and all cards — as one file. A colleague uses Import to merge it into theirs (same ids update, new ids add). The exported file is also the handoff artifact into the game repo.

Formatting

Rules and flavour text allow simple HTML: <b>6</b> for bold numbers, <br> for a line break.
