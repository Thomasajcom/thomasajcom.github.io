Something uphill?

It shouldn't be. Here are the answers to most things, and an address for the rest.

Mountains

What counts as a mountain?

Anything you think you're supposed to climb. A marathon, an inbox, a career ladder, a garage. If it makes you sigh when you think of it, it qualifies.

What's the difference between flattening and crossing?

Flattening means deciding not to climb it at all. Crossing on the flat means you did it, but the easy way, a small step at a time. Both send the mountain to the Plain, and both count as climbing avoided.

I flattened something I actually need to do.

Open the Range, switch to the Plain, tap the mountain, and choose “Stand it back up”. It returns to the range with its steps intact. No judgement.

How do I remove a mountain completely?

Open it from the Range and tap “Remove without ceremony” at the bottom.

The tools

The Lie Flat timer keeps pausing.

It only runs while the phone is lying flat, face up or face down. On a sofa cushion or in your hand it reads as tilted and pauses. A table works. So does your chest, if you breathe calmly, which is rather the idea.

What is my multiplier in the Easy Ledger?

For each thing you log, the app divides how long it took by how long you thought it would take. Your multiplier is the middle value of those, so one catastrophic afternoon doesn't skew it. It gets more honest with every entry.

How do I delete a ledger entry?

Touch and hold the entry, then choose Remove.

I gave the wrong answer to “how steep was today?”

Tap Change on the check-in card, on Today or in the Terrain, and pick again. Only today's answer can be changed. Yesterday is flat ground now.

The library

How do pages open?

By using the tools. Each closed page says what it's waiting for: a number of mountains dealt with, minutes lying flat, ledger entries, daily check-ins, or metres of climbing avoided. Today always shows the page you're closest to.

Can a page close again?

No. Once a page is open it stays open, even if you stand a mountain back up.

The subscription

How does the free trial work?

The first three days are free. If you cancel at least a day before they end, you pay nothing. Otherwise the subscription starts and renews until you cancel. Apple shows you the exact price before you agree to anything.

How do I cancel?

In the app: Today, then Settings, then “Manage subscription”. Or in iOS Settings: tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

I subscribed, but the app is asking again.

Tap Restore on the subscription screen, or “Restore purchases” in the app's Settings. Make sure the iPhone is signed in to the Apple Account you subscribed with.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds go through them at reportaproblem.apple.com. I can't issue one myself, but write to me if something went wrong and I'll help where I can.

Your data

Where are my mountains kept?

In one file on your iPhone. There is no account and no server. The privacy page has the details, and it is short.

Do they sync between devices?

No. They come along when you move to a new iPhone from a backup, but two devices won't share a range.

The reminder isn't arriving.

Check that it's on in the app's Settings, then check iOS Settings, Notifications, Flat Is Fine, and allow notifications there. The default time is eleven, when Descartes got up.
