Privacy

Privacy, on one flat page.

The short version: the app has no idea who you are, and would rather not find out.

Effective November 17, 2026 · Applies to The Descartian Way iOS app

What the app stores, and where

Everything you put into the app stays in one file in the app's own storage on your iPhone:

  • The mountains you name, who you said put them there, how steep they looked, and the small steps you divided them into
  • What you logged in the Easy Ledger: what it was, how long you thought it would take, how long it took
  • How many minutes you spent lying flat, and when
  • Your daily answer to “how steep was today?”
  • Which library pages have opened and which you've read
  • Whether you want a daily reminder, and at what time

There is no account and no sign-in. I don't run a server for this app, so none of it is sent to me. I can't see it, and I can't get it back for you if you lose it.

Motion

Lie Flat reads your iPhone's motion sensor to tell whether the phone is lying flat. It does this only while a session is running. The readings are used on the spot and thrown away. The only thing kept is the number of minutes.

Reminders

The daily reminder is optional and off until you turn it on. It is scheduled on your iPhone by your iPhone. Nothing is sent from anywhere.

Purchases

The subscription and its free trial are handled entirely by Apple. I never see your name, your Apple Account, or your payment details. Apple tells the app one thing: whether the subscription is active. Apple also gives me totals, such as how many subscriptions were sold in a country, with nothing that identifies you.

What can leave the device

  • Things you share. A share card goes wherever you send it, and nowhere else. It is made on your iPhone.
  • Your own backups. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, the app's file is part of that backup, under your Apple Account and Apple's terms.
  • Apple's analytics, if you opted in. If you turned on “Share with App Developers” in iOS Settings, Apple sends me anonymous crash reports and usage totals. That is an iOS setting, not something the app collects.

What the app doesn't do

  • No account, no email address, no profile
  • No analytics or tracking code, mine or anyone else's
  • No advertising
  • No third-party software inside the app at all
  • No selling or sharing of data, because there is none to sell

Children

The app is written for adults with too much on their list. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Deleting your data

Remove a mountain from its page in the Range, or a ledger entry by touching and holding it. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes everything it stored. There is nothing for me to delete on my side, because I never had it.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. That is done in iOS Settings, under your name, in Subscriptions.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that changes this page, the new version appears here with a new effective date. It will stay about this long.

Contact

Questions about any of this? Email support@thomasaj.com. There is also a support page, and the app is licensed under Apple's standard terms of use.
