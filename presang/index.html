How it reads

One glance tells you how ready you are.

Occasions pin the string up — a birthday, a Christmas, an anniversary — and gifts hang from them as small paper tags in that person's colour.

Idea
Planned
Given

A tag becomes real as the plan does

Dashed while it's just an idea. Tied on once it's planned. Filled in once it's given. Nothing to tick off, nothing to file — the tag simply looks like where you've got to.

Today is a bead at the centre

Breathing gently, with the past hanging to your left and the future to your right. Quiet stretches aren't empty: the string simply drapes, and a whole quiet season reads as one graceful sag.

Hold the string, slide, let go

A new occasion is pinned where your finger was. Along the top edge there's a measuring tape — drag it and the months tick past, years at a time.

Worth knowing

The small things it does quietly.

  • People are loops of thread, soonest day first, with the rhythm of every year drawn beside their name — what you gave hangs below the line, what they gave rises above it.
  • Gifts is a ledger: given and received, year by year.
  • Spot something in March — save the idea, and when that person's day comes around, Presang hands it back.
  • On someone's actual birthday their pin blooms, once a year.
  • On a quiet day the string remembers what this day looked like years ago.
  • Beyond birthdays — Christmas, anniversaries, whatever you celebrate.
  • Pick people straight from Contacts — name, birthday, photo — and they arrive already looking like themselves.
  • Rebuilt for iOS 26, and every gift logged since 2020 comes along untouched.
